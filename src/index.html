LuantiHub

The next-generation package registry and content discovery platform for Luanti (formerly Minetest) players, server operators, and mod developers.

Connecting to LuantiHub & ContentDB Mirror...

Git-Backed Community Packages

Directly submit and install mods hosted on GitHub, GitLab, and Codeberg without gatekeeping. Full open-source license enforcement ensures legal compliance and safety.

In-Game Content Tab Compatibility

Point your Luanti client directly to LuantiHub via contentdb_url in luanti.conf (or minetest.conf) to search, download, and update mods directly inside the game.

High-Availability Failover Mirror

Integrated with the contentdb.milcho.org fallback mirror. If official ContentDB undergoes maintenance, LuantiHub stays 100% online without interruption.

Aggregated Community Reviews

Read community reviews combining official ContentDB feedback with native LuantiHub comments, giving creators and players a unified voice.
