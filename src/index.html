Luanti Hub (LuantiHub) – Mods, Games & Packages for Luanti (Minetest)

Welcome to Luanti Hub (also known as LuantiHub), the open package registry, mod repository, and content discovery platform for Luanti (formerly Minetest) players, server operators, and mod developers.

Git-Backed Community Packages

Directly submit and install mods hosted on GitHub, GitLab, and Codeberg with automated mod.conf and Luacheck static verification.

In-Game Content Tab & Direct .zip Downloads

Download ready-to-drop .zip archives or point your Luanti client directly to LuantiHub via contentdb_url in luanti.conf.

High-Availability Failover Mirror

Integrated with the contentdb.milcho.org fallback mirror. If official ContentDB undergoes maintenance, LuantiHub stays 100% online without interruption.

Aggregated Community Reviews & Tags

Explore packages by official ContentDB tags, inspect dependencies and README documentation, and share community star ratings.
